About Votrite Mobil Shop

A mobile-first marketplace that helps local businesses get discovered, in their own city and around the world.

Empowering businesses. Connecting people.

Votrite Mobil Shop gives every business its own page with photos, products, deals, location and a direct link to its shop, so customers can find it in seconds from their phone.

Customers browse by category and city, search for what they need, and go straight to the business. Businesses see how many people viewed their ad and clicked through.

Help Center

Answers for customers and advertisers.

How do I find a business?

Use the search bar on the home page, pick a city, or tap a category. Every business has its own page with products, deals, location and a link to its shop.

Can I buy directly on Votrite Mobil Shop?

Not yet. Votrite Mobil Shop shows businesses and their offers; you buy from the business itself through the “Visit their shop” button.

How do I advertise my business?

Go to Advertise and post your first advertisement for free. Once it is reviewed and approved, it appears in search, in its category and in its city.

What does an additional advertisement cost?

Your first ad is free during the launch promotion. Each additional ad is $0.50 per day while it runs, and you can pause or stop it at any time.

What is a Featured Placement?

Featured Placement is $1.00 per day extra. Featured ads appear at the top of the home page with a gold “Featured” badge, get priority in search results and appear in Latest Deals.

Is this connected to VotRite voting?

No. Votrite Mobil Shop is completely separate from VotRite voting. There are never any ads on ballot pages or in the voting app.
